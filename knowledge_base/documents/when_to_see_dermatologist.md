# Warning signs — when to seek care — summary

> Summarized in our own words from Mayo Clinic dermatitis guidance for RAG grounding.
> See the full guidance at the source URL above.

## Routine professional evaluation

Consider seeing a dermatologist when a rash does not improve with basic gentle skin care, keeps returning, interferes with sleep or daily activities, or when the cause is unclear.

## Signs needing prompt attention

Seek prompt in-person care for signs such as rapidly spreading redness or warmth, swelling, pus or discharge, red streaking, severe pain, or fever accompanying a rash. These can indicate infection or another condition needing urgent evaluation.

## What to tell the clinician

Helpful details include when the rash started, whether it itches, hurts, or burns, what seems to trigger or relieve it, any new soaps, detergents, medicines, or skincare products, and whether it is spreading. A short video showing the area can also help.
